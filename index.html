quant_02 · in progress

M3 on NIFTY

Training M3, a state-event generative foundation model for market microstructure (arXiv:2608.19227), on Indian-market tick-level and order-book data. It tokenizes order events and LOB states, models them with an autoregressive transformer, and replays generated orders through a matching engine for forecasting, stress tests and market-impact what-ifs.
